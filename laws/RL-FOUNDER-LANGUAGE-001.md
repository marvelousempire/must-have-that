# RL-FOUNDER-LANGUAGE-001 — Founder language with exact truth

**Status:** required, open across all consumers

Every requirement, README, marketing statement, app label, empty state, status
and receipt summary explains the system in the Founder's way of thinking:

1. purpose;
2. human meaning;
3. what goes in;
4. what comes out;
5. who owns the truth or effect;
6. how it is proved; and
7. what the person can do next.

Plain language and exact technical identity travel together. A label may say
“The learned specialty”; its detail and receipt still say Adapter, exact base,
dataset, recipe, configuration, revision and hash. Founder language may clarify
complexity. It may not hide ownership, permission, failure, drift, an open gate
or an unproved capability.

## Verification

- README, current-state, marketing and affected UI copy use the same meaning.
- Labels have exact technical names available in detail or receipt views.
- Loading, empty, blocked, failed, recovered and completed states identify the
  real owner and next action.
- Source-only, installed and live states remain different.
- Each affected Suit receives an independent content and runtime witness.
