# RL-GOVERNED-LEARNING-CMS-001 — Governed learning through the CMS

**Status:** open must-have  
**Owner:** Briefcase CMS / Hybrid CCK records; Automata executes; Nephew preserves Family authority  
**Consumers:** ACT-v4 Suits, HermesTHAT, HelloTHAT, Pocket Spot and admitted agents

## Bootstrap English

The Family teaches once. The CMS keeps the durable truth. Every authorized
agent and Suit may use the same current version without inventing another copy.

Keep these as different governed things: a Teaching, a Knowledge Source, a
Knowledge Snapshot, a Dataset, a Training Run, an Evaluation, an Adapter, a
Promotion, a Rollback and a Receipt. Link them; do not collapse them.

Every durable input and output has a CMS/Hybrid CCK record with a stable ID,
owner, Family scope, permissions, provenance, version, status, relations,
content hash or secure owner reference, and created/updated dates. The CMS
offers authenticated REST create, read, update and archive operations wherever
the object's policy permits. A CLI or app consumes those endpoints; it does not
write a second database.

Secrets, credentials, transient tokens, temporary audio buffers, private model
weights and oversized binary payloads remain with their secure owner. Their CMS
record carries only safe metadata, policy, hash, owner reference and receipt.

Knowledge stays editable and citable. Training is a dated projection of
reviewed teaching and datasets, never the only copy of Family knowledge.
Adapters identify their exact frozen base model, dataset snapshot, recipe,
configuration, source revisions, training machine and output hash. LoRA and
QLoRA are different recipes and different versions. DeepSeek or any other model
is a candidate until hardware fit, license, evaluation, rollback and live
acceptance pass.

Train beside the active model. States are `draft`, `reviewed`, `candidate`,
`failed`, `accepted`, `active`, `superseded`, `revoked` or `rolled-back`.
Promotion is a separate approved owner action. A failed candidate cannot become
active by changing a label.

## Required verification

1. REST discovery lists the record types, methods, schemas and permissions.
2. Authorized create, read, update and archive round trips succeed for every
   durable type; unauthorized mutation refuses and writes an evidence receipt.
3. A Teaching becomes a versioned Knowledge Snapshot and cited answer without
   changing its source or Family scope.
4. A reviewed Dataset records every source relation and excludes revoked or
   unauthorized material.
5. A LoRA or QLoRA candidate trains against a frozen, exact base and reloads in
   a separate profile; base, adapter, dataset and configuration hashes read back.
6. Evaluation includes task quality, safety/approval behavior, memory and skill
   continuity, failure recovery, hardware fit and regression comparison.
7. Promotion keeps the old active route recoverable; rollback restores it and
   proves an independent reply plus one governed tool receipt.
8. The installed Suit reports owner-supplied machine, engine and model truth.
   Web proof does not prove Tauri, Swift, Pocket Spot or another machine.
9. Source, merge, CMS persistence, training output, installation and live use
   remain separate receipt states.
10. The CMS can reconstruct the complete input-to-output lineage without
    exposing credentials or private payloads.

This must-have is not cleared by schemas, fixtures, a model download, a build,
an API health check or a successful training loss alone. It clears only after
the protected release and real installed acceptance receipts exist.
